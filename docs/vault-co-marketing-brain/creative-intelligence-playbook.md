# Creative Intelligence Playbook — Vault Co

How each creative type should shape the AI Campaign Builder's output. When a creative asset is selected in the builder, the creative type drives the campaign angle, hook, copy, CTA, audience temperature, and placement strategy.

## Why Creative Type Matters

The same service can be advertised with completely different approaches depending on what creative asset is available. An owner-on-camera video demands a trust-first, personal hook. A storm damage before/after demands urgency-aware, compliance-careful copy. Using the wrong copy angle for a creative type reduces performance and creates mismatched messaging.

The Creative Intelligence system ensures the campaign draft is always shaped by what the creative can actually deliver — not just what the service theoretically needs.

## Creative Types

### 1. Owner On Camera

**What it is:** Video or photo featuring the business owner speaking directly to the audience.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold to Warm — works well for first impressions with people who have not heard of the company.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Human accountability — a real person stands behind the work
- Local presence — owner is from the community
- Expertise and confidence
- Approachability — "this is who I would be hiring"

**Campaign angle:** Trust-building. Lead with the founder story, the local connection, or a direct offer explanation.

**Best hook:** "[Owner Name] here — I've been roofing in [City] for [X] years. Here's what I want you to know before calling anyone."

**Best CTA:** "Get My Free Inspection" or "Talk to [Owner Name]"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed (primary), Instagram Feed, Reels

**Audience temperature:** Best for cold audiences who need to meet the company before trusting it.

**Compliance note:** No unverifiable claims about being the "best" or "cheapest." Any specific statistics (years in business, reviews) must be accurate.

**Retargeting use:** Ideal as first touch in a cold-to-warm sequence. Follow with testimonials or before/after content.

### 2. Before/After

**What it is:** Side-by-side or sequential images/video showing a roof or project before and after the work was completed.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold to Warm — visual proof for homeowners evaluating quality.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Real work quality
- Problem solved
- Craftsmanship visible in the outcome
- Local project reference (if neighborhood is named)

**Campaign angle:** Proof-driven. "This is the work we do in [City]."

**Best hook:** "Before we showed up. After we were done." or "Here's what a [Service] looks like when it's done right."

**Best CTA:** "See If You Qualify" or "Get Your Free Assessment"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed (primary), Instagram Feed, Reels

**Audience temperature:** Cold to warm. High social sharing potential.

**Compliance note:** Must be authentic client work. No stock photos. No competitor jobs. No AI-generated imagery. Do not imply every job looks this dramatic if variance is high.

**Retargeting use:** Excellent for mid-funnel retargeting. Use after owner-on-camera introduces the brand.

---

### 3. Testimonial

**What it is:** A homeowner or past client speaking about their experience with the company.

**Buyer intent level:** Warm — most effective for audiences already aware of the company.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Social proof from a real person
- Specific outcome or experience described
- Relatable situation (homeowner like me trusted them)

**Campaign angle:** Social proof. "Here's what your neighbors are saying."

**Best hook:** "[Client Name] in [City] had the same concern you might have right now..." or open with the most compelling quote from the testimonial.

**Best CTA:** "See How We Can Help You" or "Get Your Free Inspection"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed (testimonials perform worse in Reels context)

**Audience temperature:** Best for warm retargeting and lookalike audiences.

**Compliance note:** Testimonials must be real, obtained with consent, and not embellished or paraphrased to imply outcomes that were not experienced. Do not add claims to a testimonial that the reviewer did not make.

**Retargeting use:** Core retargeting asset. Use for website visitors and video viewers.

---

### 4. Storm Damage

**What it is:** Video or photo showing storm damage, hail impact, or wind damage on a roof.

**Buyer intent level:** Hot — highest urgency creative type. Buyers triggered by a recent weather event.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- The company understands this specific problem
- Expertise in storm and insurance work
- Speed — implies rapid response capability

**Campaign angle:** Urgency without fear. Assessment and guidance framing.

**Best hook:** "Recent storms in [City] may have damaged your roof — and most damage is not visible from the ground."

**Best CTA:** "Get My Free Storm Assessment"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed (primary), Instagram Feed, Messenger — avoid Reels for storm urgency content.

**Audience temperature:** Hot — homeowners who just experienced a storm event.

**Compliance note:** HIGHEST RISK CREATIVE TYPE. Never imply insurance will cover repairs. Never guarantee a claim will be approved. Never say the roof will be free. Only use in markets where a real storm recently occurred. Do not manufacture urgency. Flag for compliance review before launch.

**Retargeting use:** Strong for same-day or same-week retargeting after a storm event. Urgency degrades quickly — rotate after 2 weeks.

### 5. Inspection Day

**What it is:** Video of an inspector on a roof, assessing shingles, checking gutters, walking the property.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold to Warm — educates homeowners on the inspection process.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- The company is thorough and professional
- The inspection process is shown — no mystery
- The inspector is knowledgeable (shown pointing out issues and explaining them)

**Campaign angle:** Education-first. "Here's what a professional roof inspection looks like."

**Best hook:** "Most homeowners have never seen what's actually happening on their roof. Here's what our inspectors look for."

**Best CTA:** "Schedule My Free Inspection"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed, YouTube (if available), Reels

**Audience temperature:** Cold — ideal for homeowners who are curious but not yet convinced.

**Compliance note:** Do not imply that every inspection will find damage. The inspection is educational and assessment-based, not guaranteed to result in a job.

**Retargeting use:** Use early in funnel sequences to educate before making a direct offer.

---

### 6. Project Reveal

**What it is:** A polished walkthrough or showcase of a completed roofing project — new materials, clean install, proud homeowner (if consented).

**Buyer intent level:** Warm — buyers who are considering but need quality confirmation.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Pride in the work
- Clean, professional installation
- Attention to detail
- Homeowner satisfaction

**Campaign angle:** Quality and pride. "This is what your neighbors' roofs look like when they choose us."

**Best hook:** "Just finished this [Type] roof in [Neighborhood/City]. Take a look."

**Best CTA:** "Get Your Free Estimate" or "See If Your Roof Qualifies"

**Best placements:** Instagram Feed (primary), Facebook Feed, Reels

**Audience temperature:** Cold to warm.

**Compliance note:** Feature real projects only. If homeowner appears on camera, written release is required.

**Retargeting use:** Effective mid-funnel. Pair with testimonial for full proof sequence.

---

### 7. Team Photo

**What it is:** Photo or video of the crew, office team, or company culture.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold — brand awareness, not direct response.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Company size and stability
- Local employment
- Team culture and professionalism

**Campaign angle:** Brand trust. "This is the team that will show up at your home."

**Best hook:** "Meet the team behind [Company Name] — [X] people serving [City] and surrounding areas."

**Best CTA:** "Learn More" or "Get a Free Inspection"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed

**Audience temperature:** Best for cold brand-building. Not optimized for direct response.

**Compliance note:** Team members must consent to appearing in ads. Do not make claims about team size that are inaccurate.

**Retargeting use:** Low priority for retargeting. Better as top-of-funnel brand content.

---

### 8. Drone Footage

**What it is:** Aerial video of a completed project, the client's service area, or a job in progress.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold to Warm — impressive visual that conveys scale and quality.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Investment in professionalism (drone = serious company)
- Scale of completed work
- Local market presence
- Premium positioning

**Campaign angle:** Premium and professional. "We invest in doing the job right."

**Best hook:** Aerial reveal of a completed roof from above, paired with voiceover or text overlay.

**Best CTA:** "Get My Free Inspection" or "Get a Free Estimate"

**Best placements:** Reels (primary), Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed

**Audience temperature:** Cold — high visual impact for first-impression campaigns.

**Compliance note:** FAA Part 107 compliance for commercial drone operation. Do not film homes or properties without consent.

**Retargeting use:** Effective top-of-funnel brand content. Use before testimonials and before/after in a cold sequence.

---

### 9. Jobsite Walkthrough

**What it is:** Walk-and-talk video on an active jobsite — crew working, materials being installed, foreman explaining the process.

**Buyer intent level:** Warm — buyers who want to know what happens between signing and completion.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Transparency about the process
- Active crew = real company with real projects
- Expertise and attention to detail during installation

**Campaign angle:** Process transparency. "Here's what happens when we're on your roof."

**Best hook:** "Want to know what actually goes into a proper roof installation? Here's what we're doing on this job in [City] today."

**Best CTA:** "Schedule My Inspection"

**Best placements:** Reels, YouTube (if available), Facebook Feed

**Audience temperature:** Warm — buyers who are already considering and want process confidence.

**Compliance note:** Do not film identifiable homeowners or their property without consent. Avoid showing anything that could imply unsafe working conditions.

**Retargeting use:** Excellent for middle-funnel retargeting with warm video viewers.

---

### 10. Warranty Explanation

**What it is:** Owner or expert explaining the warranty coverage — materials, labor, duration, and what it protects against.

**Buyer intent level:** Warm to Hot — buyers who are price-comparing or hesitating.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Company stands behind the work
- Long-term relationship, not a one-time transaction
- Reduces perceived risk of making the wrong choice

**Campaign angle:** Risk removal. "If anything goes wrong, we'll fix it."

**Best hook:** "Most roofers won't tell you this about warranties. Here's what your warranty actually covers — and what it doesn't."

**Best CTA:** "Talk to Our Team" or "Get Your Free Assessment"

**Best placements:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed

**Audience temperature:** Warm — best for retargeting audiences who engaged but did not convert.

**Compliance note:** Only claim warranties that the company actually offers and can fulfill. Do not imply manufacturer warranty coverage unless the client has the required certification. Do not promise warranty transferability unless confirmed.

**Retargeting use:** High-value late-funnel asset for re-engaging warm but unconverted leads.

---

### 11. Q&A Clip

**What it is:** Short-form video of the owner or expert answering a specific question a homeowner commonly asks.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold to Warm — educational content that pre-answers objections.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Expertise and approachability
- Willingness to educate, not just sell
- Owner/team is responsive and helpful

**Campaign angle:** Objection pre-handling. Answer the question before the buyer even asks it.

**Best hook:** Use the question as the hook: "How do I know if my roof actually needs to be replaced?" or "What's the difference between a repair and a replacement?"

**Best CTA:** "Get a Free Assessment" or "Ask Us Anything"

**Best placements:** Reels (primary for organic reach), Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed

**Audience temperature:** Cold — great for education-first cold traffic campaigns.

**Compliance note:** Answers must be factually accurate. Do not make specific claims about insurance, pricing, or outcomes in a Q&A format.

**Retargeting use:** Useful for educating warm leads who have not converted. Address the objection most common for that lead segment.

---

### 12. UGC Style Video

**What it is:** Authentic-looking, user-generated content style video — often shot on a phone, conversational, unpolished but credible.

**Buyer intent level:** Cold — highly effective with cold traffic on mobile.

**Trust signals communicated:**
- Authenticity — feels real, not like an ad
- Word-of-mouth credibility
- Approachable and relatable

**Campaign angle:** Peer recommendation. Feels like a neighbor or friend talking about their experience.

**Best hook:** Direct to camera, conversational: "I just had my roof done by [Company] and I can't believe I waited so long to do this."

**Best CTA:** "See If You Qualify" or "Get My Free Inspection"

**Best placements:** Reels (primary), Instagram Stories, Facebook Feed

**Audience temperature:** Cold — highest scroll-stop rate on mobile placements.

**Compliance note:** If this is produced content made to look organic, it must not imply it is an actual unsponsored user review. FTC guidelines require disclosure when sponsored content is produced to look organic. Platform disclosure settings must be enabled.

**Retargeting use:** Effective top-of-funnel. Use as a hook before directing warm audiences to social proof and before/after content.

## Creative Selection Guide

| Creative Type | Best For | Avoid When |
|---|---|---|
| Owner On Camera | New markets, first-impression campaigns | Owner is not confident on camera |
| Before/After | Mid-funnel proof, retargeting | No authentic project photos available |
| Testimonial | Warm retargeting | No consented customer testimonials |
| Storm Damage | Post-storm urgency campaigns | No recent storm in target area |
| Inspection Day | Cold education campaigns | No professional inspection footage |
| Project Reveal | Quality-focused campaigns | Projects are inconsistent in quality |
| Team Photo | Brand awareness only | Direct response campaigns |
| Drone Footage | Premium brand positioning | Budget-positioned clients |
| Jobsite Walkthrough | Middle-funnel process buyers | Jobsite is not camera-ready |
| Warranty Explanation | Late-funnel objection removal | Client has weak or no warranty |
| Q&A Clip | Cold education, objection handling | Owner is not comfortable on camera |
| UGC Style Video | Cold mobile traffic | Brand requires polished/premium feel |

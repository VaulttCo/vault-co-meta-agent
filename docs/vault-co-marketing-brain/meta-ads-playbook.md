# Meta Ads Playbook — Vault Co

How Vault Co builds Meta advertising campaigns for roofing and construction clients.

## Core Principles

**Client-first** — Every campaign is built around the specific client's offer, market, buyer psychology, and competitive position. We do not recycle templates. We do not run the same campaign for every roofer.

**Service-first** — Each campaign promotes one service with one offer. We do not try to promote everything at once. Focused campaigns outperform scattered ones.

**Market-specific** — Location targeting, audience language, and creative references are local. Homeowners in Phoenix respond differently than homeowners in Cleveland. A campaign should feel like it was made for their neighborhood.

**Creative-led** — The hook comes first. The creative asset drives the campaign angle. A before/after video demands a different hook than an owner-on-camera testimonial. We shape every campaign around what the creative can actually deliver.

**Compliance-safe** — Every draft is reviewed against Meta ad policy, TCPA requirements, and insurance advertising rules before submission. No false claims. No guaranteed outcomes. No discriminatory targeting.

**Appointment-focused** — The metric that matters is booked appointments, not leads. CPL is a proxy metric. A $30 CPL that produces zero bookings is worse than an $80 CPL that books 60% of leads. We optimize for CPB (cost per booking) and booking rate.

## Campaign Types

### 1. Free Roof Inspection

**Objective:** LEAD_GENERATION via Instant Form
**Audience temperature:** Cold — reaching homeowners not actively searching
**Offer:** No-cost, no-obligation roof inspection
**Hook style:** Problem-aware ("Your roof might be older than you think") or trust-first (owner on camera)
**Primary text angle:** Address fear of wasted time or being sold. Frame as expert assessment, not sales call.
**Lead form:** Collect name, phone, email, address. Ask property type, approximate roof age, homeownership status. TCPA consent required.
**GHL follow-up:** Immediate SMS + call within 5 min. Inspection scheduled by end of call.
**Compliance watch:** Do not imply the inspection will find damage. Do not guarantee insurance will cover repairs.

### 2. Full Roof Replacement

**Objective:** LEAD_GENERATION — qualified homeowners with older or damaged roofs
**Audience temperature:** Warm — retargeting or interest-based audiences who showed prior intent
**Offer:** Free estimate or consultation
**Hook style:** Quality/proof-first — before/after, project reveal, warranty mention
**Primary text angle:** Quality, longevity, warranty. Financing available. "One call, done right."
**Lead form:** Collect name, phone, email, address. Ask roof age, current issue (leak, aging, storm), owns or rents, financing interest.
**GHL follow-up:** Immediate SMS + email. Setter calls within 5 min for appointment booking.
**Budget range:** $2,000–$5,000/mo. CPL target: $60–$100.

### 3. Storm Damage Inspection

**Objective:** LEAD_GENERATION — homeowners in recent storm zones
**Audience temperature:** Hot — triggered by weather event, high intent
**Offer:** Free storm damage assessment
**Hook style:** Urgency without fear. "Recent storms in [City] may have damaged your roof — find out before it costs more."
**Primary text angle:** Protect the investment. Most damage is not visible from the ground. We assess it for free.
**Lead form:** Collect name, phone, email, address. Ask when the storm hit, visible damage observed (yes/no), homeownership status.
**GHL follow-up:** Fastest possible. Immediate SMS + email + setter call within 5 min. Inspector dispatched within 24–48 hours.
**Compliance watch:** Do not say "insurance will cover it." Do not say "file a claim for a free roof." Do not imply guaranteed approval. Do not manufacture storm urgency when no storm occurred.
**CPL target:** $40–$70 (higher intent market).

### 4. Retargeting Homeowners

**Objective:** CONVERSIONS or LEAD_GENERATION — warm retargeting audience
**Audience:** Website visitors, video viewers (25%+), past lead form openers, engagement audiences
**Offer:** Reminder of the original offer, or a new angle (testimonial, before/after, urgency)
**Hook style:** Social proof first — "Here's what [City] homeowners are saying about us"
**Primary text angle:** Soft close. Remove friction. "Still thinking about it? Here's what to expect when you call."
**Budget:** 15–20% of total campaign budget
**Placement:** Instagram Feed, Reels, Facebook Feed. Avoid Audience Network for retargeting.
**Frequency cap:** 3–5 impressions per user per week.

### 5. Owner-On-Camera Trust Campaign

**Objective:** AWARENESS + LEAD_GENERATION hybrid
**Audience temperature:** Cold — first impression campaign
**Hook style:** Owner introduces themselves, names the city, states the offer directly
**Primary text angle:** Local, personal, honest. "I'm [Name], I've been roofing in [City] for [X] years. Here's what I want you to know before calling anyone."
**Creative requirement:** Owner must be on camera. Real face. Real name. Real location reference.
**Trust signals:** Years in business, review count, certifications, local project photos
**Compliance watch:** No unverifiable claims. If owner says "best roofer in Phoenix" — remove it.
**CPL target:** $50–$90. This campaign builds brand equity that improves all other campaigns.

### 6. Warranty Education Campaign

**Objective:** LEAD_GENERATION — homeowners with aging roofs who are on the fence
**Audience:** Homeowners 40–65, homes 15+ years old, mid-to-high HHI
**Hook style:** Education-first. "Most homeowners don't know this about their roof warranty."
**Primary text angle:** Inform before selling. Explain what manufacturer warranties cover, when they expire, and why getting an inspection now protects the investment.
**Lead form:** Collect contact info. Ask: "When was your roof last inspected?" and "Do you know if your current roof has a manufacturer warranty?"
**Compliance watch:** Do not make specific warranty claims unless confirmed for that client's materials and certifications.

### 7. Before/After Proof Campaign

**Objective:** LEAD_GENERATION — social proof driven
**Audience temperature:** Cold to warm
**Creative requirement:** Real before/after photos or video from actual client jobs. No stock. No AI-generated imagery.
**Hook style:** Visual transformation. "Before we showed up. After we were done."
**Primary text angle:** Quality and reliability. "This is the work we do in [City]."
**Compliance watch:** Before/after images must be authentic client work. Do not use competitor jobs or stock photo transformations. Do not imply every job looks this dramatic.
**Placement:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Reels. Visual-first placements.

## Budget Guidelines

| Monthly Budget | Recommended Allocation |
|---|---|
| $1,500/mo | 1 active campaign, 1 ad set, 3 creative variants |
| $2,500/mo | 2 campaigns (inspection + retargeting), 2 ad sets each |
| $3,500–$5,000/mo | 3 campaigns, 3–4 ad sets, test new creatives monthly |
| $5,000+/mo | Full funnel: cold, warm, retargeting, lookalikes |

## Performance Benchmarks

| Metric | Roofing Inspection | Replacement | Storm |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPL Target | $50–$80 | $80–$120 | $40–$70 |
| Booking Rate Floor | 40% | 30% | 50% |
| CPB Target | $150–$250 | $300–$500 | $100–$200 |
| Creative Fatigue Trigger | CTR drops 30% week-over-week |
| Budget Scale Trigger | CPB below threshold for 7+ days |
| Pause Rule | No bookings in 72 hours at target CPL |

## What We Never Do

- Run the same creative for more than 6–8 weeks without a refresh
- Ignore Meta compliance review risks in copy
- Promise results in ad copy
- Use stock photos when real project photos are available
- Target audiences in a way that could trigger Meta's housing discrimination policy
- Launch a campaign without at least 3 ad copy variants
- Skip the GHL workflow setup — ad performance is half the equation; follow-up speed closes the deal

# Vault Co — Positioning

## Who We Are

Vault Co is a premium growth partner for roofing and construction companies. We are not a cheap lead generation agency. We do not sell volume. We build revenue systems that generate booked appointments, qualified sales opportunities, and closed jobs.

## The Core Problem We Solve

Most roofing and construction companies do not have a lead problem. They have a system problem.

- Leads come in and go cold because nobody follows up fast enough
- Sales processes are inconsistent — different closers produce wildly different results
- Ad spend is wasted on the wrong audiences, wrong creative, wrong offer
- There is no backend follow-up sequence to recover lost leads
- The CRM is underused or ignored entirely

Vault Co addresses all of this. We do not just run ads. We install a full growth system.

## What We Build

**1. Meta Ad Campaigns** — Built for the specific client, market, service, and buyer. Not templated. Not recycled. Creative-led, compliance-safe, targeting the right homeowner at the right moment.

**2. GHL Follow-Up Systems** — Immediate SMS and email on lead submission. Internal notifications to the setter. AI voice follow-up if no call is made within 10 minutes. Daily nurture sequences for 3 days. Lost lead recovery. Full stop conditions once booked.

**3. Client Intelligence** — We extract structured intelligence from every client onboarding. Buyer psychology. Market research. Competitive analysis. Offer structure. Sales audit. This intelligence is injected into every campaign we build.

**4. Creative Intelligence** — We analyze each creative asset and use that analysis to shape the hook, copy angle, CTA, audience temperature, and placement strategy.

**5. Approval Workflows** — Every AI-generated campaign draft goes through human review before any ad is published, budget is changed, or workflow is activated.

## Who We Serve

- Roofing companies (residential and commercial)
- Home improvement companies (windows, siding, gutters, HVAC, solar)
- General contractors and remodelers

Ideal client: $500K–$5M annual revenue, wants to grow past word-of-mouth, has a setter or sales team to handle leads, ready to invest $1,500–$5,000/month in advertising.

## What We Are Not

- We are not a $299/month leads provider
- We are not a general digital marketing agency
- We are not a social media management company
- We are not a website design shop
- We do not guarantee leads, jobs, or revenue (no one can — and honest operators do not claim otherwise)

## Brand Voice

Premium. Direct. Operator-minded. We speak like growth partners, not vendors. We talk in terms of revenue, appointments, close rates, and systems — not clicks, impressions, or follower counts.

We treat clients like business owners making strategic decisions, not buyers shopping for the cheapest option.

## Positioning Statement

Vault Co builds the revenue systems that roofing and construction companies need to stop relying on referrals and word-of-mouth, and start generating consistent, qualified appointments from paid media — with the follow-up infrastructure to close them.

# SMS response-time → booking pattern

> Veronica · seed conversation-intelligence report

## Finding
Hot inbound leads contacted within the first hour book at a meaningfully higher rate, and
urgency-framed appointment confirmations reduce no-shows. Of 8 reviewed conversations: 2 hot
(awaiting follow-up), 2 dead (reactivation candidates), 1 no-show, ~25% booked.

## Evidence
Read-only review of lead conversations (GoHighLevel when configured; mock otherwise). Pattern
requested for validation by Vega via collaboration before escalation.

## Implication
- **Follow-up SLA:** standardize a sub-hour response on hot inbound.
- **Confirmations:** adopt urgency-framed confirmation templates to cut no-shows.
- **Reactivation:** seasonal re-engagement for dead conversations.

Drafted follow-up / reactivation / no-show-recovery messages were created for **human approval
only** — Veronica never sends. Surfaced as Command Hub recommendations.

## Objection patterns
- "Want to compare a couple quotes first" (warm)
- "Price seems high" (cold)

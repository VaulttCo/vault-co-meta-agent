# What Vault Co Should Stop Doing

Derived from the legacy GHL archive ([[Legacy GHL Messaging Lessons]]) + the Identity Core
guardrails ([[Vault Co Identity Core]]). These are anti-patterns the workforce must not repeat.

1. **Stop sending "just following up" texts.** Most-ignored message in the archive. Replace with a
   value- or specific-ask follow-up (a concrete time, a relevant detail).
2. **Stop responding slowly to hot inbound / missed calls.** Late texts miss the intent window.
   Auto-text < 2 min; human follow within 15 (human-approved).
3. **Stop over-texting in reactivation.** Daily cadence drove opt-outs. Go slower and value-led.
4. **Stop discounting on "price seems high."** Reframe to cost-per-booked-job, don't train negotiation.
5. **Stop multi-question messages.** One clear ask per text.
6. **Stop letting automation run past a lead's question.** Hand to a human (takeover rule).
7. **Stop generic agency language** ("we generate leads", "grow your business") and overhyped AI claims.

Each maps to a Command Hub recommendation (human review required). Nothing here sends or mutates GHL.

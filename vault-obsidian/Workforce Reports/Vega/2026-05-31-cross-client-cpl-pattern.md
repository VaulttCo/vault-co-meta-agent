# Cross-client CPL pattern

> Vega · seed intelligence report

## Finding
Roofing clients show a meaningful CPL spread; the lowest-CPL account's creative + audience
structure appears transferable to lagging accounts.

## Evidence
Cross-account comparison of `client.stats.cpl` across active accounts (Phase 1 deterministic
analysis). Storm-season creatives correlate with lower CPL.

## Implication
Front-loading budget ~2 weeks ahead of forecasted storms and replicating the top performer's
structure could compress CPL on lagging accounts. Surfaced as a Command Hub recommendation for
human review — **no action taken automatically**.
